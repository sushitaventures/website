**Phase:** Section 06 — Business Discovery & Strategic Positioning  
**Status:** Working Discovery Framework  
**Last Updated:** 2026-11-22  

---

## 1. Research Protocol

Competitor research must be strictly grounded in the **actual operating geography, market stage, and service scope** of the business once confirmed.

### Strict Guardrails:
- **No Premature Assumptions**: Do not analyze random global portals (e.g., Zillow, Rightmove) or residential brokerages (e.g., Sotheby's International Realty) unless confirmed relevant to the founders' specific business model.
- **Differentiation over Imitation**: Identify industry conventions to subvert or refine, rather than copying competitor visual templates or marketing copy.
- **Fact vs. Interpretation**: Separate observable competitor features from analytical commentary.

---

## 2. Benchmark Evaluation Dimensions

When verified competitors are identified, each will be analyzed across:
1. **Brand Positioning & Value Proposition**: Fiduciary advisor vs. transactional broker vs. listing aggregator.
2. **Service Presentation & Depth**: How do they present institutional services like industrial, land, or distressed asset advisory?
3. **Trust & Credibility Signals**: Client logos, transaction track records, press citations, team credentials, licensing clarity.
4. **Digital Experience & Motion**: Typography, whitespace, performance, visual elegance, mobile UX.
5. **Conversion & Enquiry Funnel**: Friction, form complexity, privacy assurances.
