- **Negative Space Rule:** High-end aesthetic driven by negative space, refined typography, and authentic media rather than flashy sales widgets.

1. **The Discerning HNWI / Family Office:**

2. **The Corporate Occupier / Institutional Fund:**

3. **The Developer / Land Investor:**

4. **The Stressed Asset Owner / Institutional Creditor:**
