## 1. Overview & Classification Framework

Every piece of material provided during the discovery phase (ChatGPT links, conversation exports, WhatsApp excerpts, handwritten notes, PDFs, competitor references, and images) is cataloged here.

### Finding Classification System:
- **[Confirmed Fact]**: Explicitly verified and supported by primary source evidence.
- **[Founder Preference]**: A stated personal opinion, aesthetic taste, or editorial preference.
- **[Business Decision]**: Formally approved operational, architectural, or commercial decision.
- **[Strategic Hypothesis]**: Professional interpretation or potential positioning angle requiring validation.
- **[Recommendation]**: Professional strategic advice proposed by the digital advisory team.
- **[Open Question]**: Information that remains unknown, ambiguous, or unconfirmed.
- **[Contradiction]**: Two or more sources that appear inconsistent or in tension.
