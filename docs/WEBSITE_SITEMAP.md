## 2. Detailed Page Specifications

### 2.1 Home (`/`)
- **Purpose:** Serve as the brand's primary digital flagship, establishing luxury prestige, architectural credibility, and immediate clarity of capability.
- **Primary Audience:** High-net-worth investors, commercial enterprises, property developers, and prospective sellers.
- **Visitor Intent:** Understand what sets this firm apart, gauge credibility, explore advisory scope, and find a direct pathway to discuss a transaction.
- **Business Objective:** Position the brand as premier market authorities; drive qualified enquiry conversions across commercial, residential, and specialized asset classes.
- **Expected Content Categories:**
  - Full-screen cinematic hero sequence with brand statement.
  - Core philosophy and value proposition.
  - Curated pathway to the 7 specialized services.
  - Credibility indicators (transaction scale, institutional track record, founders' statement).
  - Dynamic highlight zone (reserved for candidate homepage offer or featured architectural film).
  - Primary conversion trigger (inquiry CTA).
- **Expected Calls to Action:** "Explore Services", "Schedule Consultation", "Inquire Privately".
- **Relationships:** Links to all service subpages, About Us, and Contact Us.
- **Information Still Required:** Confirmed brand name, official tagline, hero video assets, founding narrative, approved key metrics.

### 2.3 Commercial Service Page (`/services/commercial`)
- **Purpose:** Detail commercial real estate advisory, acquisitions, dispositions, and corporate leasing.
- **Primary Audience:** Corporate occupiers, institutional funds, commercial investors, retail developers.
- **Visitor Intent:** Source Grade-A office spaces, retail complexes, or commercial investment opportunities.
- **Business Objective:** Generate high-value commercial purchase or mandate enquiries.
- **Expected Content Categories:**
  - Commercial asset classes covered (Grade-A Office, Retail, Tech Parks).
  - Advisory approach (valuation, tenant representation, investment yields).
  - Case studies / illustrative transaction profiles (without compromising confidentiality).
  - Dedicated commercial enquiry prompt.
- **Expected Calls to Action:** "Submit Commercial Mandate", "Request Commercial Portfolio".
- **Relationships:** Child of Services; links to Contact Us (with pre-selected category `Commercial`).
- **Information Still Required:** Operating micro-markets, average ticket size, asset class focus.

- **Expected Calls to Action:** "Confidential Asset Consultation", "Request Distressed Property Audit".
- **Relationships:** Child of Services; links to Contact Us (pre-selected category `Sick Property`).

### 2.11 Contact Us (`/contact`)
- **Purpose:** Dedicated private communication hub, hosting the formal luxury enquiry intake workflow.
- **Primary Audience:** Active leads across all 7 service verticals.
- **Visitor Intent:** Send a direct enquiry, schedule a private consultation, or obtain office coordinates.
- **Business Objective:** Maximize qualified lead capture with zero cognitive friction.
- **Expected Content Categories:**
  - Structured enquiry form (Name, Phone, Email, City, Looking For selection).
  - Private advisory office coordinates, concierge phone, direct email.
  - Discretion & privacy assurance statement.
- **Expected Calls to Action:** "Submit Private Enquiry", "Call Advisory Desk".
- **Relationships:** Terminal conversion destination linked from all pages.
- **Information Still Required:** Physical office address, official concierge phone, inquiry notification destination email.
